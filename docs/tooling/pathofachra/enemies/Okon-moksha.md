# Okon-moksha

A gathering of Okonic bodies bound by a fleck of divine refraction. An engorged star-eater. Pursues self-birth through the animalistic devouring of light A quivering bulk slouches in the expanse of dying light.

**Found in:** Ruler of the Eaten Star
