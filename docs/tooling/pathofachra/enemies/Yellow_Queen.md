# Yellow Queen

Masked regent sent to the gate of horn. Exiled long ago from a broken tower. Awaits the 'wandering king' of the star-path. Presides over monstrosities of thought A faraway voice whispers "Her mask is raised"...

**Found in:** Ruler of the Mind-warren
