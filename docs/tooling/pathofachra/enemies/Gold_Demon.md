# Gold Demon

A demon of radiant yellow light. Guardian of the mind-warren and the gate of horn. Said to be born from "the room at the end of the mind" You hear voices calling. Strange thoughts...

**Found in:** General Void Nemeses, Mind-warren Nemeses
