# Death Knight

A grim warrior-mage exiled from the gate of death. Pursues the secret 'ritual of emancipation' at the end of the world You see a pile of slain Flesh Priests with their skin removed.

**Found in:** Final Land Nemeses

**Resistances:** Slash 50%, Blunt 50%, Pierce 50%, Death 90%, Astral -100%
