Tactics

The Slagh Astromancer poses very little threat on low cycles, with its low Hit of only 180; on higher cycles, however, it is one of the most dangerous guardians due to its scaled Speed. On low cycles, the Slagh Astromancer is easily dispatched by a pilgrim with a decent amount of Armor/Block/Dodge and high enough Speed. On high cycles, it poses as a hard counter to any builds that rely upon adjacent attacks, since they have almost no opportunity to reach it due to its Disruption and Astral Dancing, all the while being pelted by its ranged attacks. It does not have Area Attack, so most ally-summoning builds should be able to defeat it without much difficulty. Its Prescience poses a threat against builds that rely on standing still. Since it can teleport and attacks at range, the Slagh Astromancer is unlikely to lost its Meditate stacks. Against builds that rely on standing still but have not managed to raise their damage potential enough to defeat it quickly, this could be devastating.
