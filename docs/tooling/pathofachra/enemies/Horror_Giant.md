The Horror Giant is one of the guardians of the Obelisk, a god at the end of the world. This creature of immense size formed of volatile unearthly matter presides over fear.

Tactics

The Horror Giant possesses a variety of abilities that make it a threat for many playstyles, especially those relying on adjacent attacks. When engaging with it in melee, take heed of its Vigilance, which will trigger an attack against you as you approach, and its high Armor: if you do not have enough Hit to reliably penetrate it, you will face counter attacks due to Bully. The Horror Giant's weakness is its low Speed and Accuracy. If fought at range by a player with decent speed, the Horror Giant becomes quite helpless. A player with high Dodge can easily avoid its attacks altogether, although it will still be guaranteed to inflict its status effects on each attack due to Force; out of these effects, Corrosion and Doom are especially dangerous since they make its attacks deal damage even if they are avoided.
