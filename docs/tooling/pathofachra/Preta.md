Pretas are enemies with randomly composed attributes and abilities, meant to be rare and dangerous. Their presence increases as you approach the Obelisk and go beyond, as does their power. They also appear on the path of Dust.

In-game description: "A "hungry ghost" from the land of dust. Takes on a random distorted form and wields chaotically assembled powers."
