Path of Achra is the eponimous main setting of Path of Achra, a long island in the eastern World-Ending Sea, home to the Towers and the sky-piercing Obelisk.

History

According to the Uhrata lore, the Path was first visited by their cult, who rowed ribbon-hung ships from a cove of lost Shuppurak. The island itself dates to the First Lowering of Waters; its towers, Green, Violet and Red, were not created by hand of folk. Incrusted of light, the towers were incumbent to existence of Achra at that age. The 'Victory' lore quoted above implies that it is possible to impact the rest of the world through taking control of the towers, suggesting that they act as 'pillars' of being itself.

The Obelisk, the same source implies, is much younger. Described as 'shimmering', it pierces the Astral Wall, allowing pilgrims to physically enter the Star-Path should they scale it. It is implied that the Obelisk's rise was one of the portents of the coming apocalypse.

The abundance of ruins on the Path suggests an ancient civilization, unknown at the time of the first pilgrimage. The game does make mention of people living on the Path, although their population is scarce. It is unclear whether they are natives or pilgrims.

At the time of the Pilgrim's journey, the Path is full of monsters and contesting factions embroiled in violence, each pursuing their own goals.
