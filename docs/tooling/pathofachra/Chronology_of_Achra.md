Creation Myth

In the beginning, Ikshana, the Divine Eye, first gazed into the void; its will became stars. Blinded by the brightness of the stars, Ikshana recoiled, and the stars rose above the earth following its gaze, forming the astral wall. At that point, other gods took part in the shaping of Achra: Hadad lowered the waters, baring the lands of Achra for habitation, creating the Lightning") element. The Green Mother") created Life") in her sacred grove; in disgust for the brightness of the stars, she disgorged the filth-valleys, creating Poison"); after being 'divided from life', she created Death"), her corpse becoming the gate of death. Phoenix became Achra's sun, possessing the Fire") element and resolving to defend the astral wall from invaders from the dust-void. The process of creation yielded the wheel-shaped Rainbow of Creation, also known as the goddess Spectra"), who presided over birth and the still-living sea. Anroth, a divine warrior, struggled upon the rainbow wheel against the demons of chaos until it disappeared. Agara, the god of storms, birthed Auzom, Ragna, Sita and Rabal, dual-elemental lesser gods of disasters.

Ancient Earth

The early life of Achra is called 'of ancient earth' and exists mostly through fossils.

Civilization appeared in several places throughout the early aeons of Achra. The first mortals had bodies of stone and inhabited mostly the mountainous regions; of them, only Volkites have survived. One such being was the first elementalist, who build Achra's first temple in what was to become the temple-lands, and dedicated it to Spectra"). At about the same time, Mardok populated the middle lands with ox-horned people, who established a flourishing polis and became fearsome warriors; their civilization was destroyed by a plague, sanctified as the god Takhal. The filth-valleys were populated by Oozes, poison elementals, who eventually became allies of the Brud. The dawn marshes were populated by Saurians. The Isle of Giants was full of large wildlife and people. Other races of ancient origins included the Imps and the Koszmar, born at the bottom of the world.

Destruction of the Sacred Grove

The Sacred Grove of pine trees is created by the Green Mother in the beginning of time as a haven for her children, the first life. That includes gods Ninhurs, Humbaba and Eresh. An ancient king cuts down the grove and killed Green Mother's") children with a weapon known as the Pine Cleaver in search of Glory; this act of deicide lead to the appearance of Ferods") and Arba, embodying the grove's rage at its destruction. This also leads to the change in personalities of the slain child gods, with Ninhurs and Humbaba, once gentle, becoming vengeful and developing a hatred for civilization and kings. Eresh, after her mother's death, takes to mourning inside her corpse, the gate of death. The Briarfolk, a race of plant-people, had since awaited the return on of the sacred grove.

Conquest of the Burning Lands

The war-queen, presumably a Naqui from the temple lands, lead an army of Fire")-users west, into the fertile plains inhabited by the peaceful Agnites and Volkites. This war turns the fertile plains into the inhospitable burning lands; the war-queen's ascent to the throne of the marble palace as well as her reign are said to be replete with atrocities and tyranny. She establishes the excorites as a secret police to weed out undesirables.

Extinguishing of the First Light

The element of Fire"), which belonged to Phoenix, the sun-Goddess of Achra, is stolen by the child-God Azar, who uses it to resurrect his brother Agni. In revenge, Phoenix inspires the war-queen of the burning lands to eradicate their followers. While she succeeds in wiping Azar's cult off the face of Achra, Agni's followers escape to the deep forest, becoming known as Topheths. After the war-queen's death, her civilization becomes lost. The loss of Fire presumably leads to Phoenix's death, eventually plunging Achra into an eternal night.

Eating of the Stars

Dwellers of the astral wall become compelled to eat of the stars' flesh, which causes horrific mutations and damages the fabric of reality, but also grants strange, chaotic powers. In order to combat the star-eaters, Phoenix establishes a troop of firebird-riders, who successfully banish several culprits, among which are Gods Fawdaa and Oros. The surface of the eaten stars spawns strange abominations, embodying the symbol-shape of the eye, that being Okokorpi") and the Okon Moksha").

Establishment of Lost Shuppurak

Lost Shuppurak is established in the middle lands of Achra, likely as a successor to Mardok's stone polis. They worship Pallas, the Goddess of tactics and Yu, the Goddess of jade. Yu's body is used to carve weapons and armor for Shuppurak's troops, which grants them unprecedented might. Their first king establishes the order of Paragons. Their army defeats the ancient Cave Worm"), a battle which flattens the coastal plains. When the Bull-Giant comes to Achra, they try to placate him with gifts of weapons before a nameless champion slays him.

Insurrection of the Keliots

A cult of Keliots"), serpentine hunter-princes, leads a rebellion against the Serpent-Kings of the dawn marshes and succeeds, taking over the throne. Their regime is punctuated by tyrannical quashing of dissent, which leads the Saurians to flee from the dawn marshes.

Conquest of the Path of Achra

The last king of Shuppurak, known as the 'wandering king', engages in many raids across Achra; he also establishes a gladiator arena in his polis, which brings to prominence many war-cults. After meeting with the Prince in Red, who has visions of the Path of Achra, an island of towers that uphold the world, the king builds a fleet of ships, eager to explore it. The first cult to ever set foot on the Path are the Uhrata. Eventually, many cults land on the Path, where they wage war and commit violence, enticed by the power of the towers.

Second Rising of Waters

The king of Shuppurak successfully takes over the towers of Achra and summons the screaming Obelisk, which pierces the astral wall, allowing the king to reach the star-path. During his journey in the stars, he meets the Craft-Queen"), an Arjana mage who had arrived some time prior by breaking through the astral wall with her blue tower. The king then encases Ikshana in stone and creates the Astrolith on top of it, ascending to godhood as King of Many Colors"). He instates the Prince in Red as the ruler of the Blood Void and the Craft-Queen") the regent of the Mind Warren. To rule the towers of Achra, he instates some of its most abominable cults. He then uses his powers to make the world-ending sea rise, looking to bring apocalipse to all of Achra.

The Tablets of the Imp Vizier") are directed towards the King, being a bid to curry his favor.

This is when the events of Path of Achra take place.

Second Lowering of Waters

In the canonical ending of Path of Achra, the Pilgrim manages to defeat the King of Many Colors") and cause the world-ending sea to recede. Soon after, the events of Lands of Achra") take place, with many new factions forming upon the remnants of the pre-flood world, vying for land and control.
