The Prince in Red rules the Blood-Void and acts as the boss on its final floor. He is the semi-divine master of the inner blood, governing the pull of blood, blood magic and violence.

Abilities

Tactics

The Prince in Red is a formidable force in melee combat. His cycle-scaled Accuracy, Hit and Speed allows him to output the highest attack damage bar none, further increased by his Cutting Blood and Impact abilities against all enemies in his range. His true danger, however, lies in his Exult ability, which deals a fraction of his high Hit to units in a 2 tile range. This all but defangs any builds that go for summoning numerous allies while increasing the Prince's own stats due to Deadly Feast. Since the Prince also possesses Cruel Feast, he will recover significant amounts of HP when his allies are killed; take heed of that.

The Prince's weakness is his low range. As long as you are able to defeat him at range quickly enough before he can hit you or have allies with enough HP and blood resistance to stall him, he should not present much of a threat.

History

[](/wiki/File:AstralFeat.png)

O Seeker! Heed!

An abyss of time divides the old Achra from us. All said and shown hereafter is subject to the distortion of ages that nothing evades.

The Prince in Red is mentioned in several lore entries throughout the game. Once a mortal of Achra, the prince-consort of the war-queen of the burning lands, he came across the Mask of Frenzy in the depth of the marble palace. Donning the Mask inflamed his spirit and filled his mind with visions of the star-path; guided by these visions, he embarked on the Path of Achra"). During his quest for the Path, the Frenzied Ones were established as a war-cult mimicking his combat style. It is implied that the Red Axe was a weapon the Prince used to wield at that time.

At some point, the wandering king") of lost Shuppurak and the Prince met and became allies. After the King ascended as the King of Many Colors, he granted the Prince in Red the dominion over inner blood in the Blood-Void").

The Pilgrim meets the Prince as a barely-humanoid creature of elemental Blood, wielding a fascimile of the Red Axe fused with the Frenzied Ones' ritual staff, the wing of light, and a blood-wrought scimitar. He is accompanied by mindless Blood Wretches spawned from his korpus, perfect warriors who know nothing but bloodlust.

According to Ulfsire, the spirit inhabiting the Mask of Frenzy was a failed incarnation of Phoenix, 'a disembodied frenzied force' brought into existence by Warlocks of the Burning Lands who sought to comprehend the glory of Phoenix in its totality. The fiery intoxication that brings forth the Frenzied One magic stems from this spirit; this is why it is said that the Frenzied Ones have 'touched the wing of light'.

Trivia

- Whereas the King of Many colors has many similarities to Gilgamesh, the Prince in Red could be viewed as a reference to Enkidu, being one of his most trusted retainers and a mighty warrior.
