An abyss of time divides the old Achra from us. All said and shown hereafter is subject to the distortion of ages that nothing evades.

Blood refers to an element and a path in the Achra universe. It is also referred to as 'gore' and is represented by the color red. This element represents violence, pain and self-harm, but also desire and will to live. Infliction of blood-based damage is referred in Path of Achra as 'rending', that is, inflicting rending pain. As a rule, enemies incapable of feeling pain such as wretches and golems have high Blood resistance.

Path of Blood

Though the "Path of Blood" is widely preached by gore cults who roam the mountains and deserts of Achra, blood-magic is traced to stranger realms

Deep in the tunnel-lands, above the infernal plane, the red-furred Irga congregate in festivals of blood, where they sacrifice groaning beasts to the "inner blood" that "surges beyond the dark"

In the temple-lands, Blood Mages often speak of this place; a gyre of blood rotating through the stars, itself a great red star, curled like a snake, that presides within the blood-pull of life, a font to which all history vanishes

Nameless Blood God

One of the sources of Blood powers and lore is the Nameless Blood God who resides beneath the earth. Among his notable worshippers are the Gil-Elga") and its Giliots") of the underground swamps and the Secret Tunnel Church. The Blood Revenant") enemies are said to have been 'animated by the nameless god of blood'. The Gore Idols are also dedicated to it, and serve as earlygame enemies, blood fountains spewing out Gore Nymphs.

The Secret Tunnel-Church

The Secret Tunnel-Church is a religion based in the depths of the underground tunnel-lands, characterized by its followers' red masks. A majority of its flock are presumably Morlocks; its clergy are called Pain Clerics. The Blood-Drinkers were a sect of it before being eradicated by a Flame Knight of the temple lands. While secretive and uninterested in spreading their word, this religion has spawned several sects among surface dwellers, most notably the Executioners and the Suwag. The Priest class is also dressed in their ritual garb.

Inner Blood

The Inner Blood is a mystical concept in the Achra universe; Blood Mages and a variety of other aspirants call upon its powers. The Inner Blood is inherent to any living being with a desire for self-preservation; with correct procedure, its vitrea can be tapped and used to heal or attack. Many blood-based abilities manifest as a 'ruddy mist' or a crimson substance which has no relation to actual blood; it is rather an elemental manifestation of the Inner Blood, pure pain which is hostile to all life. The Inner Blood is also related to 'the pull of blood', an instinctual desire for consumption of life and mortification of flesh, a 'life-thirst', an obscessive yearning. Notably, Humbaba's unique Blood Rage effect seems to work though inciting with the blood's craving.

The Inner Blood is ruled by the Prince in Red of the Blood Void, an eldritch heavenly body akin to a nebula or a galaxy. The Blood Void is referred to as 'the font to which all history vanishes', highlighting how violence and eradication, both domains of blood, have destroyed an unnameable number of civilizations, leaving nary a memory; some lore also implied that Blood is capable of remembering things that were destroyed, among them the Blood-Drinkers' magic, which is said to 'wait deep in the pull of blood'.

The Inner Blood is worshipped by many cults all over Achra, most notably, by the Irga civilization.

The Irga

The Irga are a civilization of red-furred humanoids who reside in the tunnel lands above the infernal plane. Their religion is characterized by their blood-festivals, where they make sacrifices of beasts, overseen by Irga Priests"). One such sacrifice was a night-serpent who taught the Damunja their art; its skin and bones were fashioned into a large swathe of items, that is, the Shaakhara, Bone Knife, Bone Shield, Bone Skirt, Bone Pakarna, Sange Axe and the Ruddy Fist. The Irga in Path of Achra often use Living Gold") for their armor and armaments, which makes them the only culture besides Hobgoblins to do so. In Lands of Achra"), the Irga are present as a playable faction and have swapped Living Gold") for steel.

In Path of Achra, the Irga are represented as three kinds of hostile units, Irga Guards"), Irga Priests") and the Irga Knight"). Items that originate from the Irga culture include the Robe of Gore and the Crimson Staff as relics of the blood-festivals as well as the Crimson Helm, a relic of 'an Irga blood-lord'.
