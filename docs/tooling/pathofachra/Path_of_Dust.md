The Path of Dust is an endless game mode that can be played through with any of your characters that have been registered in the Maqbara, this is done by either achieving victory with them or by dying. Once you click Start in the bottom right corner, you can begin your run with the selected character.

You cannot Save within the Path of Dust and the highest floor you reach with the character is listed on their entry in the Maqbara.

Each Floor you ascend gives Amplification to the enemies by (Floor Number)², Preta enemies show up much more often in The Path of Dust, Pretas are enemies with randomly composed attributes and abilities.

Ending a run rewards your account with Glory if you make it to Floor 5 or later, however, the amount is quite small compared to the main game.
