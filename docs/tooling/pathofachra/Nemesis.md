Path of Achra Nemeses

Barring forbidden places, which draw a single enemy type from the general Void nemesis pool, all areas on the Path of Achra draw nemeses from one of four common pools.

Generic areas house two distinct nemeses, while the towers and the Obelisk house four, in addition to their guardians.
