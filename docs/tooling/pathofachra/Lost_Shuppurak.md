Lost Shuppurak is a city-state in Achra's Age of the First Receding of Waters. Considered one of the greatest forces of that time, its history proved to be integral to the fate of the whole world.

Chronology

[](/wiki/File:AstralFeat.png)

O Seeker! Heed!

An abyss of time divides the old Achra from us. All said and shown hereafter is subject to the distortion of ages that nothing evades.

Torquoise Shuppurak

Shuppurak's two main periods could be delineated through reviewing the change of their divine patron. Once it was Pallas, the goddess of defense and craftiness, who gave them torquoise metal that was their mark. It was their first war-king who founded the order of Paragons, banner-carriers and tacticians who anointed their hoplites for battle. At that time already, Shuppurak was quite cosmopolitan, with the Paragons tapping upon a wealth of combat traditions to create their strategies. The Paragons' war on the Cave Worm was said to have flattened the coastal plains. It was also then that Shuppurakan gladiatorial traditions have begun to be established, bringing warriors from all over Achra to test their might against one other.

Jade Shuppurak

It is unknown when exactly it was that Yu, the poisoned stone, became Shuppurak's dominant religion. Remnants of Yu's teachings speak of her in three forms: a rider of light who fell from beyond the stars, a jade polis beneath the earth and the 'poisoned stone' of Shuppurak. The latter form, it particular, was a jade statue that was enshrined in the city and inspired many religious and martial traditions. Jade hewn from Yu's body was used to create weapons and priestly garb, and Kings themselves wore it. It was in this period that the Wandering King, after spearheading numerous successful campaigns reaching as far as the Crystal Warren, chose to abandon his people in favor of searching for immortality, which is what earned him that title.

Lost Shuppurak

Most texts refer to the nation as such; while many knew the city as a ruin, its strong culture persisted far beyond its fall. Warriors and Mubarizuns, Galas and Secutors all traced their traditions back to Shuppurak. While the coastal plains have since been rife with people who bandied Shuppurak's warrior-lore, no attempt was made to rebuild the fallen city.

Age of Distortion

After the Flood, Strani survivors took inspiration from Shuppurak's Torquoise period and learned their metal-lore, establishing the Shuppuri. Meanwhile, the Jade period was reborn as the Yu-abra, a resurrected form of Yu's church.

Trivia

1. Shuppurak's name is based off the name of a Sumerian text, "Instructions of Shuruppak". The name Shuruppak refers to a Sumerian king who is stated to the the last king before the deluge and the father of Utnapishtim.
2. Linguistically and culturally, lost Shuppurak borrows from Ancient Roman and Ancient Chinese cultures, although there are some Ancient Greek and Arabic words as well. Secutor and Samnite were Ancent Roman gladiator types. The Gala are a reference to the Ancient Roman cult of Bacchus. Dianmai comes from the Chinese "点脉", referring to the "touch of death" martial art that incorporated hitting the opponent's pressure points to gain advantage.
