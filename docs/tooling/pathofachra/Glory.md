Glory has several meanings in Path of Achra.

[](/wiki/File:Topbarcyclops.png)

[](/wiki/File:Topbarcyclops.png "Enlarge")

Glory accumulates as a yellow bar under hit points and above speed bar. Current level of glory is denoted by * and a number to the right of Divine Interventions.

On the Path, Glory is gained by entering floors and slaying enemies. When a pilgrim's Glory rises, they gain a stat and a power point.

On higher cycles, Glory gains are increased, and therefore power and stats are gained faster, which means on higher cycles pilgrims can get Prestige Classes much earlier.

Tip: Towers will always get you the most Glory. Forbidden Places have enemies that grant more Glory when slain, but only have one floor.

Glory is also a score recorded after a run. Every 20 run Glory unlocks a new starting option (culture, class or religion). If there are no more starting options locked, it simply accumulates with no limit.
