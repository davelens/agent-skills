# Robe of Gore

A red-sigiled robe. Fashioned by an Irga arch-priest during the festivals of blood...

**Slot:** Chest armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 1 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Blood 30%

## Effects

On dealing damage to an ally:

- deal 50 Blood damage to 1 enemy in a 3 tile range

On self-damage:

- apply 3 Bleed to 1 enemy in a 3 tile range
