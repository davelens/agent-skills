# Xyston

A heavy battle-spear washed ashore from the island of giants...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 180 | 100 | Pierce | 1 | 1 | 8 | 5 | No |

**Resistances:** none

## Effects

On dealing Physical damage:

- increase the damage by 20% of your Armor

"Physical" damage is Slash, Blunt and Pierce

On a unit stepping adjacent to you:

- perform an extra attack against the unit

Extra attacks use your Main-hand weapon and do not count as an "initial attack"
