# Agni Mantle

A mantle once dedicated to the destroyer-god Agni. Stolen from a ruined temple in the burning lands...

**Slot:** Chest armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 40 | 8 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Fire 15%, Astral 15%

## Effects

On shrug off:

- apply Scorch to the attacker
- deal Fire damage to yourself

Self-damage = your Strength

Scorch applied = 10 * your Strength

Self-damage is unaffected by resistance / increase

On prayer:

- heal yourself

Heal = 10 * your Strength
