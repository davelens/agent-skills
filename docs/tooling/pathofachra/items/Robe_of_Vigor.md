# Robe of Vigor

A green robe worn by a frenzied summoner of the deep forest...

**Slot:** Chest armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 1 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Poison 20%, Blood 20%, Death 20%

## Effects

Summoned allies gain +10% of your max Life

On raising your Vigor:

- gain +50 max Life

On stand still:

- all allies gain +50 max Life
- heal all allies for 50
