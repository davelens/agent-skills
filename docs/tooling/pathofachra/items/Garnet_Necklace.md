# Garnet Necklace

A necklace worn by a volcano-dwelling sage. Abandoned during the Surtmir conquests...

**Slot:** Chest armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 1 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Fire 50%, Lightning 50%

## Effects

On dealing Fire / Lightning damage:

- apply 1 Inflame to yourself
- remove Scorch and Freeze from yourself
