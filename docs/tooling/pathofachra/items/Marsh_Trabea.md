# Marsh Trabea

A trabea abandoned by a serpent-sage of the dawn marshes...

**Slot:** Chest armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 1 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Poison 50%, Death 50%

## Effects

On dealing Death / Poison damage:

- apply 1 Evasion to yourself
- remove Sickness and Doom from yourself
