# Agni Helm

A horned helmet once dedicated to the destroyer-god Agni. Stolen from a ruined temple in the burning lands...

**Slot:** Head armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 15 | 8 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Fire 10%, Astral 10%

## Effects

On being attacked:

- apply Inflame to yourself
- deal Fire damage to yourself

Self-damage / Inflame applied = your Willpower

Self-damage is unaffected by resistance / increase

On prayer:

- heal yourself

Heal = 10 * your Willpower
