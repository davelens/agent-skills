# Ill Manica

An armguard forged from otherworldly scales that glow with strange inner starlight...

**Slot:** Arm armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 20 | 3 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Poison 20%, Astral 20%

## Effects

On attack with a bare fist:

- deal Poison damage to the target

Damage = 10 * Willpower

On dealing Poison damage:

- apply 2 Meditate to yourself
