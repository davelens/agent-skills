# Viper Dao

A curved sword infused with poison magic. Forged by a hermit-smith of the dawn marshes...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 180 | 120 | Slash | 8 | 1 | 3 | 2 | No |

**Resistances:** Poison 15%

## Effects

On attack:

- deal Poison damage in a path to 2 enemies in a 3 tile range

Damage = 6 * Speed
