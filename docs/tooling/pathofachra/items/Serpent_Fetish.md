# Serpent Fetish

A fetish that invokes the great serpent-nests of the dawn marshes...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50 | 50 | Poison | 7 | 1 | 1 | 5 | No |

**Resistances:** Poison 25%

## Effects

On prayer:

- summon 3 Serpents

Serpents are slithering poison-fanged reptiles

On kill / summon:

- deal Poison damage in a path to 1 enemy in a 4 tile range

Damage = 5 * your Dexterity
