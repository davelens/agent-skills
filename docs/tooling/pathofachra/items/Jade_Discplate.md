# Jade Discplate

A glimmering disc that once adorned the warriors of lost Shuppurak...

**Slot:** Chest armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 30 | 5 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Poison 15%, Blood 15%, Death 15%, Psychic 15%

## Effects

Gain Armor equal to the total stacks of all effects on self

"Effects" are temporary conditions such as Sickness, Poise, etc
