A helmet worn by a legendary bheith nochti during his adventures in the stars...

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 50 | 15 | 0 |

**Resistances:** none

- +5 Speed

On attack:

- deal Astral damage to an adjacent enemy

Damage = 300% of your Main-hand Hit
