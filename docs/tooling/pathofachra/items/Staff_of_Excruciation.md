# Staff of Excruciation

A violet staff of pain adorned with an infolded sigil. Once carried by the 'wandering king' of lost Shuppurak...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50 | 50 | Astral | 7 | 5 | 1 | 5 | No |

**Resistances:** Blood 25%, Astral 25%

## Effects

On summon:

- apply 1 Agony to yourself

On ally attack:

- the ally deals Astral damage to itself equal to 100% of its max Life
- you repeat the damage against the attacked unit
