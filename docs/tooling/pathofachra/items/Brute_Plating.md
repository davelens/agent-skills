A thick and brutish chestplate. Worn by the legendary bull-giant of the coastal plains...

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 80 | 9 | 1 |

**Resistances:** Slash 30%, Blunt 30%

## Effects

Per point of Encumbrance:

- deal +5 Physical damage

"Physical" damage is Slash, Blunt and Pierce
