# Earth Staff

A magic staff abandoned by an earth-mage of the temple-lands...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50 | 60 | Blunt | 7 | 4 | 1 | 5 | No |

**Resistances:** Slash 25%, Pierce 25%, Blunt 25%

## Effects

On stand still:

- deal Blunt damage to all units in a 4 tile range
- apply 3 Poise to yourself

Damage = 5 * your Encumbrance
