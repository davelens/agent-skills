# Jade Pilum

A glimmering spear once wielded by the sentries of lost Shuppurak...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50 | 50 | Pierce | 6 | 4 | 3 | 5 | No |

**Resistances:** Psychic 15%

## Effects

On initial attack, for each separate effect on self:

- perform an extra attack against the target

Extra attacks use your Main-hand weapon and do not count as an "initial attack"

"Effects" are temporary conditions such as Sickness, Poise, etc
