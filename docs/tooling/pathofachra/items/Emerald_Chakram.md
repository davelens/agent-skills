# Emerald Chakram

A snake-calling chakram. Wielded by the last serpent-king of the dawn marshes against the Keliot usurpers...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 70 | 70 | Poison | 10 | 2 | 1 | 1 | Yes |

**Resistances:** Poison 15%

## Effects

On killing an enemy:

- a random ally gains +20 Speed
- summon a Serpent

Serpents are slithering poison-fanged reptiles

Total summons limited by your Willpower
