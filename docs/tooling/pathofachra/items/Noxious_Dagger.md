# Noxious Dagger

A golden dagger sacred to the Brud filth-rites...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 200 | 120 | Pierce | 7 | 1 | 1 | 1 | No |

**Resistances:** Poison 40%

## Effects

On attack:

- apply 2 Sickness to the target

On apply Sickness:

- deal Poison damage in a path to the target

Damage = stacks of Sickness on the target * 10
