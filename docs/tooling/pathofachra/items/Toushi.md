# Toushi

A green cap worn by an ancient missionary of the filth-valleys...

**Slot:** Head armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | 1 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Poison 10%

## Effects

On stand still:

- apply 5 Sickness to 1 enemy at any range
- deal Poison damage to all enemies with Sickness

Damage = 10 * target Sickness stacks
