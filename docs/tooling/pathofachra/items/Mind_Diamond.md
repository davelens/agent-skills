# Mind Diamond

A glinting head-crystal worn by master-servants of the yellow church...

**Slot:** Head armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | 1 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Psychic 10%

## Effects

On stand still:

- apply 5 Repulsion to yourself
- perform a Hit against 1 enemy at any range

Hit damage = stacks of Repulsion on self

This hit bypasses all defense and uses your Main-hand damage type
