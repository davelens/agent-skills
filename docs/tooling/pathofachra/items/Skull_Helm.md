# Skull Helm

A helm crafted from the skull of a fallen bare-chested raider. Consecrated by the Lochra priesthood...

**Slot:** Head armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 5 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Death 15%, Psychic 15%, Lightning 15%

## Effects

+2 stacks of any Doom applied by you

On dealing Death / Psychic / Lightning damage:

- apply 1 Doom to the target
