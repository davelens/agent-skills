# Mask of Frenzy

A bright mask worn by a cursed war-prince...

**Slot:** Head armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 1 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Psychic -50%

## Effects

On initial attack, per Inflame stack on self:

- perform an extra attack against the target

Extra attacks use your Main-hand weapon and do not count as an "initial attack"

On attack:

- apply 2 Inflame to yourself
- deal Fire damage to yourself and the target

Damage = 3 * stacks of Inflame on self

Self-damage is unaffected by resistance / increase
