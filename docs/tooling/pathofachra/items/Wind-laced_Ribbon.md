# Wind-laced Ribbon

A ribbon once brandished by a legendary Amir of the Eastern desert; it is said her Nomads could outrun the wind...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50 | 10 | Slash | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | No |

**Resistances:** none

## Effects

+10 Speed

On attack:

- a random ally gains Speed
- deal Slash damage in a path to the furthest enemy

Speed gain = 20% of your Speed

Damage = 10 * your Speed
