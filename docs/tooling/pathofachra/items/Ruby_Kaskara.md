# Ruby Kaskara

A brass-hilted straight sword. Once wielded by the war-queen of the burning lands...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 200 | 100 | Slash | 6 | 1 | 3 | 2 | No |

**Resistances:** Fire 15%

## Effects

On attack:

- deal Fire and Pierce damage in a path to the target

Fire damage = 10 * Strength, Pierce damage = 10 * Dexterity
