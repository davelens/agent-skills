# Sun Mask

A shining mask treasured by the lost sun cult whose vanished believers are said to have pursued the 'first light' beyond the astral wall...

**Slot:** Head armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 1 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Fire 15%, Astral 15%

## Effects

On being attacked:

- deal Astral damage to the attacker
- repeat with Fire damage
- apply Blind to the attacker

Blind applied = your Willpower

Damage = 5 * stacks of Blind on the target
