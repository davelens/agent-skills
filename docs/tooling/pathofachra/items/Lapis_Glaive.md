# Lapis Glaive

A shimmering turquoise lance. Once held by the craft-queen of the blue tower that now drifts shattered in the outer stars...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 200 | 150 | Pierce | 15 | 1 | 6 | 7 | Yes |

**Resistances:** Astral 25%

## Effects

On adjacent attack:

- apply 5 Meditate to yourself
- deal 100 Pierce damage in a straight line starting from the target
- repeat with Astral and Lightning damage
