# Dread Sash

A sash dedicated to an imprisoned cave-god of death...

**Slot:** Leg armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | 1 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Slash 10%, Pierce 10%, Blunt 10%, Death 10%

## Effects

On dealing Physical damage:

- 25% of the damage is dealt again as Death damage to adjacent enemies

"Physical" damage is Slash, Blunt and Pierce
