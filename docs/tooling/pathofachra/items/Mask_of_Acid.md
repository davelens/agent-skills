# Mask of Acid

A face-mask of dull yellow. Said to be the death mask of the entomed Heliot king...

**Slot:** Head armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 1 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Fire 10%, Poison 10%

## Effects

+3 stacks of any Corrosion applied by you

On being attacked:

- apply 7 Corrosion to the attacker
- deal Fire damage to the attacker

Damage = attacker Corrosion stacks * 5
