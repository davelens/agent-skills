# Jade Bracers

A pair of jade bracers once worn by the arch-summoner of lost Shuppurak...

**Slot:** Arm armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | 1 | 0 |

**Resistances:** none

## Effects

On game turn, if enemies live, for each separate effect on self:

- summon a Jade Soldier

Jade Soldiers are resistant minions conjured from the glint of jade
