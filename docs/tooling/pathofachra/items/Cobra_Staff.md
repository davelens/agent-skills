# Cobra Staff

A deathly staff. Once belonged to a Brud snakedancer of the dawn marshes...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 70 | 80 | Poison | 2 | 4 | 1 | 5 | No |

**Resistances:** Poison 25%

## Effects

On stand still:

- deal Poison damage to all enemies in a 3 tile range

On dealing Poison damage:

- deal Death damage in a path to 1 enemy in a 3 tile range

Damage = 5 * your Willpower
