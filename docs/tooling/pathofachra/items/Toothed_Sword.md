# Toothed Sword

A cursed red blade that emits the sound of gnashing teeth. Appeared long ago on the edge of the blood-void before the eating of the stars...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 70 | 170 | Slash | 2 | 1 | 7 | 3 | No |

**Resistances:** Blood 15%

## Effects

On kill:

- deal Blood damage to the closest enemy equal to 50% of your Main-hand Hit
- deal 50 Blood damage to yourself

Self-damage is unaffected by resistance / increase
