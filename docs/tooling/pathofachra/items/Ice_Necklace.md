# Ice Necklace

A shimmering blue necklace of frost. Worn by an Arjana priest of the frozen waste...

**Slot:** Chest armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | 1 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Ice 30%

## Effects

On apply Freeze:

- deal Ice damage in a path to the target

Damage = stacks of Freeze on the target * 10
