# Agni Skirt

A metallic skirt once dedicated to the destroyer-god Agni. Stolen from a ruined temple in the burning lands...

**Slot:** Leg armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 30 | 10 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Fire 10%, Astral 10%

## Effects

On attack:

- apply Meditate to yourself
- deal Fire damage to yourself

Self-damage / Meditate applied = your Dexterity

Self-damage is unaffected by resistance / increase

On prayer:

- heal yourself

Heal = 10 * your Dexterity
