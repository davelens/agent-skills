# Dread Helm

A helm dedicated to an imprisoned cave-god of death...

**Slot:** Head armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 30 | 4 | 1 |

**Resistances:** Slash 10%, Pierce 10%, Blunt 10%, Death 10%

## Effects

On attack:

- perform a Hit against 1 enemy at any range

Hit damage = 25% of your Main-hand Hit

This hit bypasses all defense and uses your Main-hand damage type
