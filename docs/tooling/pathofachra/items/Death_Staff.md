# Death Staff

A magic staff abandoned by a death-mage who wandered the frozen waste...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50 | 50 | Death | 7 | 5 | 1 | 5 | No |

**Resistances:** Death 25%

## Effects

On stand still:

- deal Death damage in a path to 2 enemies in a 3 tile range

On heal:

- deal Death damage to adjacent units

Damage = 50 + (50 * # of allies)
