# Lightning Gloves

A pair of storm-blue gloves. Worn by an exalted sorcerer who called the first thunderdrakes to Achra...

**Slot:** Arm armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | 1 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Lightning 15%

## Effects

On step:

- deal Lightning damage in a path to 1 enemy in a 2 tile range

Damage = 10 * your Speed
