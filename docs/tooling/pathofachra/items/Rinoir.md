# Rinoir

A knife carried by a master duelist of the coastal plains...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 300 | 140 | Pierce | 5 | 1 | 1 | 1 | No |

**Resistances:** none

## Effects

- perform an extra attack against the attacker

Extra attacks use your Main-hand weapon and do not count as an "initial attack"
