# Forest Mask

A face-mask of deep green. Belonged to a vine-sage who wandered the once-endless forests of the West...

**Slot:** Head armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 1 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Poison 10%, Blood 10%, Death 10%

## Effects

+2 stacks of any Entangle applied by you

On an ally attacking an enemy:

- you apply 1 Entangle to the target
