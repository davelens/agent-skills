# Burning Mask

A face-mask of fiery red. Worn by a high excorite of the war-queen during her conquest of the burning lands...

**Slot:** Head armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 1 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Fire 15%

## Effects

+3 stacks of any Scorch applied by you

On kill:

- apply 2 Scorch to all enemies in a 4 tile range
