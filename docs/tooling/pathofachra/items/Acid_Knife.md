# Acid Knife

A brass dagger sacred to the Heliot dance of ablation...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 200 | 120 | Pierce | 7 | 1 | 1 | 1 | No |

**Resistances:** Fire 40%

## Effects

On attack:

- apply 2 Corrosion to the target

On apply Corrosion:

- deal Fire damage to all adjacent units

Damage = stacks of Corrosion on the target * 10
