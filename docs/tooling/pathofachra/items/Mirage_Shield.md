# Mirage Shield

A shield of wavering light. Bears distorted scripture of the yellow church...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50 | 50 | Psychic | 20 | 1 | 1 | 6 | No |

**Resistances:** none

## Effects

On block / stand still:

- summon a Psychomorph

Psychomorphs are fragile projections of your weapon; they inherit Hit and Accuracy from your Main-hand
