# Jade Veil

A shifting green veil worn by the 'wandering king' of lost Shuppurak when he vanished from Achra...

**Slot:** Head armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 3 | 0 |

**Resistances:** none

## Effects

On being attacked:

- perform a Hit against the attacker

Hit damage = total stacks of Effects on self

This hit bypasses all defense and uses your Main-hand damage type
