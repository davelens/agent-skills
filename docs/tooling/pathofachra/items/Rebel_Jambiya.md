# Rebel Jambiya

A jeweled dagger once brandished by a nameless rebel-priest against the 'wandering king' of lost Shuppurak...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 200 | 100 | Pierce | 7 | 1 | 1 | 1 | No |

**Resistances:** Fire 20%, Poison 20%, Blood 20%, Death 20%

## Effects

On attack:

- double the target's lowest-stack harmful effect
- apply 10 Scorch to the target
