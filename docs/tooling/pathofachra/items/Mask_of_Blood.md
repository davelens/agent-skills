# Mask of Blood

A face-mask of blood-red. Worn by a famous executioner who wandered the coastal plains...

**Slot:** Head armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 1 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Blood 25%

## Effects

+3 stacks of any Bleed applied by you

On being attacked:

- apply 2 Bleed to the attacker
- heal yourself

Heal = attacker Bleed stacks
