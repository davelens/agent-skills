# Beast Visage

A heavy mask containing the spirit of a beast. Worn by a legendary berserker of the deep forest...

**Slot:** Head armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 20 | 15 | 0 |

**Resistances:** none

## Effects

If chest armor empty:

- +15% all resistances
- -50% Armor
- % penalty applied after all other bonuses
- -2 Inflexibility

On attack, if chest armor empty:

- apply 8 Berserk to yourself
