# Ice Staff

A magic staff stolen from a shattered ice temple during the Surtmir conquests...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50 | 50 | Ice | 10 | 5 | 1 | 5 | No |

**Resistances:** Ice 25%

## Effects

On stand still:

- deal Ice damage to enemies in a 3 tile range
- repeat against all enemies with Freeze

Damage = 10 * your Willpower
