**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100 | 30 | Blunt | 40 | 1 | 7 | 6 | No |

**Resistances:** Fire 15%, Poison 15%

## Effects

- apply Corrosion to the attacker
