# Fire Gloves

A pair fire-red gloves. Worn by the dead war-queen of the burning lands...

**Slot:** Arm armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | 1 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Fire 15%

## Effects

On attack:

- apply Scorch to the target
- summon a Burning Blade

Scorch applied = your Willpower

Burning Blades are flying spirits of fire that burn enemies

Total summons limited by your Willpower
