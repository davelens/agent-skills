# Ice Gloves

A pair of ice-blue gloves. Worn by an Inuga warrior-king of the high glaciers...

**Slot:** Arm armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | 1 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Ice 15%

## Effects

On attack / stand still:

- apply Freeze to adjacent enemies
- summon a Living Ice

Freeze applied = your Willpower

Living Ice are conscious spires of Ice that freeze enemies

Total summons limited by your Willpower
