# Shesha Jambiya

A curved knife sacred to Shesha phalangites. Bestows high repute in the clay polis of the underground sea...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 200 | 60 | Pierce | 7 | 1 | 1 | 1 | No |

**Resistances:** Astral 15%

## Effects

On attack, if your total Weapon Size is less than 5:

- ignore the target's Armor and Block

On hit:

- deal Astral damage to all enemies in a 2 tile range

Damage = 20 * Dexterity
