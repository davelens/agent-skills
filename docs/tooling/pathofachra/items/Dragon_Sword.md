# Dragon Sword

A flaming greatsword imbued with the rage-breath of a dragon. A long-lost relic of the burning lands...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 150 | 250 | Slash | 5 | 1 | 10 | 7 | Yes |

**Resistances:** Fire 25%

## Effects

On attack, if two-handing:

- apply 10 Inflame to yourself
- deal Fire damage to all units in a 4 tile range

Damage = 10% of your Main-hand Hit
