# Doom Blade

A withering blade abandoned by a death-king after performing a sacred ritual of emancipation...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 150 | 150 | Slash | 5 | 1 | 8 | 5 | Yes |

**Resistances:** Death 25%

## Effects

On attack / hit:

- apply 5 Doom to the target
- deal Death damage to all units in a 2 tile range

Damage = 10 * target Doom stacks
