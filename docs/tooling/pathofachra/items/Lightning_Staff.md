# Lightning Staff

A magic staff of flashing light. Once belonged to a prodigious thunder priest of the storm temples...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50 | 50 | Lightning | 9 | 5 | 1 | 5 | No |

**Resistances:** Lightning 25%

## Effects

On stand still / step:

- deal Lightning damage in a path to 2 enemies at any range

Damage = 2 * your Speed
