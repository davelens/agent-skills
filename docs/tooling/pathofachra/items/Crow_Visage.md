# Crow Visage

A heavy mask containing the spirit of a crow. Worn by a reclusive mage of the 'eaten stars'...

**Slot:** Head armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 9 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Death 20%, Astral 20%

## Effects

If chest armor empty:

- -50% Block
- -50% Armor
- -50% Hit
- % penalty applied after all other bonuses

On enemy death, if chest armor empty:

- perform all "stand still" actions

On stand still:

- deal 25 Astral damage to yourself

Self-damage is unaffected by resistance / increase
