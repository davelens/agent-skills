# Rat Banner

A flag of the rat-flourishers. A mercenary emblem of the rat-men...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 120 | 30 | Blunt | 1 | 1 | 3 | 3 | No |

**Resistances:** none

## Effects

On entrance / game turn:

- summon a Rat-Man Flourisher

Rat-man Flourishers are zealous fighters; whenever a Flourisher dies, you will summon a Rat-man Peltast

Total summons limited by your Willpower
