# Ragna Axe

An axe once wielded by a Surtmir general who dared to invade the infernal plane...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100 | 300 | Ice | 10 | 1 | 15 | 7 | Yes |

**Resistances:** Fire 20%, Ice 20%

## Effects

On attack:

- deal Fire damage to 1 enemy at any range
- repeat with Ice damage

Damage = 100% of your Main-hand Hit
