# Inu Greatsword

A greatsword enchanted with ice. Forged by an Inuga warrior of the high glaciers...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 140 | 160 | Slash | 20 | 1 | 6 | 5 | Yes |

**Resistances:** Ice 25%

## Effects

On attack, per point of Inflexibility:

- deal Ice damage in a path to 2 enemies in a 3 tile range

Damage = 5 * your Encumbrance
