# Crimson Plating

A blood-streaked chestplate of wild crimson....

**Slot:** Chest armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 30 | 8 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Blunt 20%, Blood 50%

## Effects

On dealing Blood / Physical damage:

- heal yourself for 5
- remove Bleed from yourself

"Physical" damage is Slash, Blunt and Pierce
