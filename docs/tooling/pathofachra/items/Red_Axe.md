# Red Axe

A greataxe of red-hued iron. Once belonged to a cursed war-prince...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 60 | 160 | Slash | 1 | 1 | 8 | 6 | Yes |

**Resistances:** Blood 25%

## Effects

On killing an enemy:

- heal yourself
- perform 3 extra attacks against random adjacent enemies

Heal = 5 * your Strength
