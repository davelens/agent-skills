# Arbestus

A mass of living vines that empower the arms of their wielder. Enchanted in the deep forest by a Bramble Lord...

**Slot:** Arm armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 5 | 0 |

**Resistances:** none

## Effects

On attack / stand still:

- perform a 10 damage Hit against 1 enemy in a 2 tile range
- repeat for each bare fist

This hit bypasses all defense and uses your Main-hand damage type
