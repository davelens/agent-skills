# Cosmic Flail

A flail of bursting light. Holds the indignant rage of an eaten star...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 120 | 170 | Blunt | 10 | 1 | 10 | 5 | No |

**Resistances:** Astral 25%

## Effects

On kill:

- deal Astral and Blunt damage to all units in a 1+ tile range

Damage = 10% of killed unit's max Life

Range = your attack range
