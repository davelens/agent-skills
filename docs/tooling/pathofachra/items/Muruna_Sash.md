# Muruna Sash

A rippling sash worn by a famous Mubarizun of the salt waste...

**Slot:** Leg armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 20 | 4 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Slash 20%

## Effects

If unencumbered:

- -6 Inflexibility

On step:

- perform an extra attack against an adjacent enemy

Extra attacks use your Main-hand weapon and do not count as an "initial attack"
