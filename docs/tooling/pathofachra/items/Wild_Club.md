# Wild Club

An enormous club wielded by ogres of the deep forest...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 80 | 300 | Blunt | 1 | 1 | 10 | 5 | Yes |

**Resistances:** none

## Effects

On attack, if two-handing:

- perform a Hit against 1 adjacent enemy

Hit damage = 30% of your max Life

This hit bypasses all defense and uses your Main-hand damage type
