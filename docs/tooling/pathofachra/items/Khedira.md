# Khedira

A dull cap that once belonged to an arch-priest of the gate of death...

**Slot:** Head armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | 1 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Death 10%

## Effects

On stand still:

- deal Death damage to the closest enemy

Damage = 25 + (25 * # of allies)

On an ally attacking an enemy:

- you apply 2 Doom to the target
