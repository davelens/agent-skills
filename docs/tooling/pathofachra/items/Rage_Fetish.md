# Rage Fetish

A skull-adorned stick divinely attuned to the berserkergang. Sacred to priests of the deep forest...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50 | 50 | Blunt | 5 | 1 | 1 | 5 | No |

**Resistances:** Slash 25%, Blood 25%

## Effects

On prayer:

- deal Slash damage in a path to one enemy at any range
- apply 5 Berserk to yourself

Damage = 10 * stacks of Berserk on self
