# Ruby Necklace

A necklace of protective flame. Stolen by a demon from the dead war-queen of the burning lands...

**Slot:** Chest armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 1 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Fire 20%

## Effects

On being attacked:

- deal Fire damage in a path to 2 enemies at any range
- apply Inflame to yourself

Damage = 10 * your Willpower

Inflame applied = your Willpower
