# Lizard Visage

A heavy mask containing the spirit of a lizard. Created as a disguise by the hermit-smith of the dawn marshes...

**Slot:** Head armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 40 | 20 | 1 |

**Resistances:** Fire 20%, Poison 20%

## Effects

If chest armor empty:

- -50% Dodge
- -50% Speed
- % penalty applied after all other bonuses
- double Hit bonus gained from Strength
- double dealt damage bonus gained from Willpower

On kill, if chest armor empty:

- heal 50
