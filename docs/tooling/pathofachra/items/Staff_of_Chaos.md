# Staff of Chaos

A trembling staff of violent energy. Once held as proof of authority by demon-kings...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50 | 50 | Astral | 7 | 5 | 1 | 5 | No |

**Resistances:** none

## Effects

On stand still, if enemies live:

- deal 1-300 Astral damage to yourself
- deal Fire damage in a path to 1 enemy at any range
- repeat with Death, Blood and Astral damage

Damage = random amount 1-100 * WIL

Self-damage is unaffected by resistance / increase
