# Taurus Helm

A mighty horned helm. Forged by the war-king of lost Shuppurak as a tribute to the rampaging bull-giant...

**Slot:** Head armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 35 | 8 | 1 |

**Resistances:** Blunt 10%

## Effects

On adjacent shrug off:

- perform an extra attack against the attacker

Extra attacks use your Main-hand weapon and do not count as an "initial attack"
