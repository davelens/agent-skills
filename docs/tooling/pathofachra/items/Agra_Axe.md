# Agra Axe

A greataxe pillaged from an abandoned Volkite temple. Inhabited by a sacred violet-hued flame...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100 | 200 | Fire | 7 | 1 | 8 | 6 | Yes |

**Resistances:** none

## Effects

On dealing Fire damage, if two-handing:

- 200% of the damage is dealt again as Astral damage to an adjacent enemy
