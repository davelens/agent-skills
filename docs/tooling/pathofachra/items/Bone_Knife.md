# Bone Knife

A dagger crafted from the fang of a fallen night-serpent...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 180 | 120 | Pierce | 5 | 1 | 1 | 1 | No |

**Resistances:** Blood 40%

## Effects

On attack:

- apply 2 Bleed to the target

On apply Bleed:

- deal Blood damage to 2 adjacent enemies

Damage = stacks of Bleed on the target * 10
