# Crystal Rod

An icon of astral power. Stolen from the crystalline warren by the 'wandering king' of lost Shuppurak...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 70 | 20 | Astral | 2 | 2 | 1 | 1 | No |

**Resistances:** Astral 30%

## Effects

On dealing Astral damage:

- 25% of the damage is dealt again to 1 enemy at one less range
