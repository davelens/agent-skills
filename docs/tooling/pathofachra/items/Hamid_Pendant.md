# Hamid Pendant

A yellow amulet of corrosion. A symbol of power among Heliots of the salt waste...

**Slot:** Chest armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | 1 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Fire 15%, Poison 15%

## Effects

Deal +5 damage per stack of Corrosion on self

On game turn, if enemies live:

- deal Fire damage to yourself
- double the stacks of Corrosion on yourself
- apply 5 Corrosion to yourself

Self-damage = stacks of Corrosion on self

Self-damage is unaffected by resistance / increase
