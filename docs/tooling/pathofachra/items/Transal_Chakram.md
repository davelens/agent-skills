# Transal Chakram

A chakram wielded by a void-wandering knight...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 70 | 70 | Slash | 10 | 2 | 1 | 1 | Yes |

**Resistances:** Astral 15%

## Effects

On hit:

- teleport the target to a random location
- apply 5 Stasis to the target

On self / any unit teleport:

- deal 50 Astral damage to 1 enemy at any range
