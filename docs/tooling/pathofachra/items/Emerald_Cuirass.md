# Emerald Cuirass

A heavy emerald-adorned chestplate. Forged long ago by a noble serpent-king of the dawn marshes...

**Slot:** Chest armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 70 | 9 | 1 |

**Resistances:** Pierce 30%, Poison 30%, Blood 15%, Death 15%

## Effects

On shrug off:

- summon 2 Serpents

Serpents are slithering poison-fanged reptiles

Total summons limited by your Willpower
