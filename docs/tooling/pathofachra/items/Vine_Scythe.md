# Vine Scythe

A scythe fashioned from the ever-living wood of a sacred forest...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 120 | 140 | Slash | 15 | 1 | 5 | 7 | Yes |

**Resistances:** Pierce 20%, Poison 20%

## Effects

On attack / hit:

- deal Slash damage to the target
- apply 5 Entangle to the target

Damage = 15 * target Entangle stacks
