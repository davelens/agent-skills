# Fire Scythe

A glittering orange scythe wielded by an arch-excorite of the burning lands...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100 | 170 | Slash | 5 | 1 | 5 | 7 | Yes |

**Resistances:** Fire 30%

## Effects

On attack / hit:

- deal Slash damage to the target
- apply 5 Scorch to the target

Damage = 15 * target Scorch stacks
