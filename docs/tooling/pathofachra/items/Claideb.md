# Claideb

A bronze sword sacred to bare-chested raiders of the deep forest...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 130 | 180 | Slash | 5 | 1 | 3 | 2 | No |

**Resistances:** none

## Effects

On initial attack, per empty armor slot:

- perform an extra attack against a random adjacent enemy

Extra attacks use your Main-hand weapon and do not count as an "initial attack"
