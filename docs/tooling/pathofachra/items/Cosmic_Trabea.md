# Cosmic Trabea

A trabea worn by a prodigious yellow priest who was exiled to the stars...

**Slot:** Chest armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 1 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Astral 50%, Psychic 50%

## Effects

On dealing Astral / Psychic damage:

- apply 1 Repulsion to yourself
- remove Stasis from yourself
