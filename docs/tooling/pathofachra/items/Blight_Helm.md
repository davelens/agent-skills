# Blight Helm

A helm forged for the last doomed blight-prince of the gate of death...

**Slot:** Head armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 20 | 6 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Death 15%

## Effects

On attack, if the target has Doom:

- summon a Cadaver

Cadavers are naked undead that deal vile Death damage

Total summons limited by your Willpower

On being dealt damage:

- apply 5 Doom to 1 enemy in a 2 tile range
