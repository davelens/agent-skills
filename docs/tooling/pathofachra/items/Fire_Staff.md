# Fire Staff

A magic staff abandoned by a fire-mage who wandered the burning lands...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50 | 50 | Fire | 8 | 5 | 1 | 5 | No |

**Resistances:** Fire 25%

## Effects

On stand still:

- deal Fire damage to 5 enemies at any range

On kill:

- deal Fire damage to 1 enemy in a 2 tile range

Damage = 5 * your Willpower
