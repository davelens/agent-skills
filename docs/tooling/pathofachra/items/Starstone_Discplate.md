# Starstone Discplate

A void-forged disc that once decorated the chest of a mighty Kashra of the star-path...

**Slot:** Chest armor

| Armor | Encumbrance | Inflexibility |
|---|---|---|
| 30 | 5 | 0 |

**Resistances:** Astral 20%, Psychic 20%, Lightning 20%

## Effects

On self / any unit teleport:

- apply 5 Poise to yourself
- apply 5 Meditate to yourself

Effect Poise: per stack: +5 Armor, +5 Block, +10 Accuracy

25% removed on step
