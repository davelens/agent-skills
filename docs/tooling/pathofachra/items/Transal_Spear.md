# Transal Spear

A spear wielded by a void-wandering knight...

**Slot:** Weapon/Shield

| Accuracy | Hit | Type | Block | Range | Encumbrance | Weapon size | Area if two-handing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 180 | 140 | Pierce | 8 | 1 | 3 | 5 | No |

**Resistances:** Astral 25%

## Effects

On stand still:

- teleport to the closest enemy and perform an extra attack against the target
- apply 5 Stasis to yourself

Extra attacks use your Main-hand weapon and do not count as an "initial attack"
