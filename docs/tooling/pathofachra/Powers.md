Powers are the core skills the Pilgrim can learn to grow in strength and defeat enemies. There are 10 different elements to choose from, and the player can choose up to seven different powers from up to three different elements. Acquiring a power requires skill points, which are gained when the player’s Glory rises. Investing skill points in each element also increases the corresponding Resistances for the player.

Contents

- 1 Martial
- 2 Fire
- 3 Lightning
- 4 Poison
- 5 Death
- 6 Ice
- 7 Astral
- 8 Psychic
- 9 Life
- 10 Blood

- deal 5 [](/wiki/File:Psychic.png "Psychic Damage")Psychic to yourself,
- repeat with [](/wiki/File:Blood.png "Blood Damage")Blood damage
  
  - Self-damage is unaffected by all resistance / increase

On self-damage:

- deal Psychic damage to all enemies in a 2 tile range
- apply [](/wiki/File:Repulsion.png "Repulsion Icon")Repulsion to yourself

Damage = 20 * skill level

Repulsion applied = skill level
